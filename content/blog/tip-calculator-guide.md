[Tip Calculator](/calculators/tip-calculator) is the tool for one job: calculate tip amount and split the total bill among multiple people. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Tip Calculator

Enter the bill, the tip percent, and how many people are splitting it.

Tip Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Tip Calculator

On an 84 bill, 15% is 12.60, so the total is 96.60. Split by 3, each person owes 32.20 before any extra rounding.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Check whether tax is already inside the bill you typed. Tipping on tax or on the pre-tax amount is a choice the form should make obvious.

## Where the Tip Calculator stops

It splits the number you entered. It does not know who ordered what.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
