[Text Diff Checker](/text-tools/text-diff-checker) is the tool for one job: highlight additions and deletions between two blocks of text. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Text Diff Checker

Paste the text you want changed. The box is the whole input.

Text Diff Checker runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Text Diff Checker

Added and removed marks are against the two boxes, line by line or word by word as the tool says. A line that only lost a space can show as changed. That is a real change to a parser.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

For a transform, run it and spot one line. For a count, count one sentence yourself and compare.

## Where the Text Diff Checker stops

Text Diff Checker sees the text in the box. It does not open a file on your computer until you paste or upload.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

It is there to highlight additions and deletions between two blocks of text. The form on the [Text Diff Checker](/text-tools/text-diff-checker) page is the place you do that.
