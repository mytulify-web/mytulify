[Color from Image](/color-tools/color-from-image) is the tool for one job: pick and extract colors directly from an uploaded image. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Color from Image

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Pick and extract colors directly from an uploaded image.

Color from Image runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Color from Image

Pick and extract colors directly from an uploaded image. Copy the value into the place you will use it, because a picker on this page and a monitor elsewhere can disagree.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the Color from Image stops

Color from Image does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Color from Image do?

It is there to pick and extract colors directly from an uploaded image. The form on the [Color from Image](/color-tools/color-from-image) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Color from Image result is usable?

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

### What should I not trust the Color from Image to decide?
