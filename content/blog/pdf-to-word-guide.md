[PDF to Word](/pdf-tools/pdf-to-word) is the tool for one job: convert a PDF into an editable DOC/DOCX document. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the PDF to Word

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Convert a PDF into an editable DOC/DOCX document.

PDF to Word runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the PDF to Word

The Word file is an editable reconstruction. Columns, headers, and tight layouts can shift. Read a page with a table before you send the DOC.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the PDF to Word stops

PDF to Word writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
