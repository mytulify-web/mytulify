[Extract Images from PDF](/pdf-tools/extract-images-from-pdf) is the tool for one job: pull all embedded images out of a PDF file. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Extract Images from PDF

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Pull all embedded images out of a PDF file.

Extract Images from PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Extract Images from PDF

This pulls images that are embedded as images. A scanned page may be one big image. A chart drawn as vectors may not come out as a picture at all.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Extract Images from PDF stops

Extract Images from PDF writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
