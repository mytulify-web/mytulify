[Flip Image](/image-tools/flip-image) is the tool for one job: mirror an image horizontally or vertically. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Flip Image

Use the original image. Mirror an image horizontally or vertically.

Flip Image runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Flip Image

Horizontal flip is a mirror. Text in the image will read backwards. Vertical flip turns it upside down. The file's pixels change. The original is untouched.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the Flip Image stops

Flip Image changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

It is there to mirror an image horizontally or vertically. The form on the [Flip Image](/image-tools/flip-image) page is the place you do that.

### What should I not trust the Flip Image to decide?
