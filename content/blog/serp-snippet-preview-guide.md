[SERP Snippet Preview](/seo-web-tools/serp-snippet-preview) is the tool for one job: preview how a page appears in Google results with pixel-width checks. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the SERP Snippet Preview

Bring the URL, the HTML, or the keyword list the form asks for.

SERP Snippet Preview runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the SERP Snippet Preview

The pixel width of a title, not only the character count, decides what gets cut off. A wide letter such as W costs more than an i. Rewrite until the title you need is visible.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the real page source or Search Console and confirm the change landed. A result on this page is not a ranking.

## Where the SERP Snippet Preview stops

SERP Snippet Preview does not control a search engine. It prepares or checks the input you gave it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
