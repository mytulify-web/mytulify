[GIF Maker](/image-tools/gif-maker) is the tool for one job: create animated GIFs from multiple images in your browser. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the GIF Maker

Use the original image. Create animated GIFs from multiple images in your browser.

GIF Maker runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the GIF Maker

The animation's frame order is the order of the images you added. Delay is the time each frame stays. A short delay looks smooth and makes a larger, busier file.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the GIF Maker stops

GIF Maker changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the GIF Maker do?

It is there to create animated GIFs from multiple images in your browser. The form on the [GIF Maker](/image-tools/gif-maker) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the GIF Maker result is usable?

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

### What should I not trust the GIF Maker to decide?

GIF Maker changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.
