[GIF to WebP](/image-tools/gif-to-webp) is the tool for one job: convert GIF animation frames to WebP images. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the GIF to WebP

Start from the original GIF file, not a screenshot of it.

GIF to WebP runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the GIF to WebP

Animated WebP can keep the motion in one file and often weighs less than the GIF. Play it once to confirm the frame timing survived.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download at the size you will use. Check transparency, text, and the file size against the original.

## Where the GIF to WebP stops

GIF to WebP converts the file. It does not recover detail the source never had.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
