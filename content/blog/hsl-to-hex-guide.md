[HSL to HEX](/color-tools/hsl-to-hex) is the tool for one job: convert HSL color values to a HEX code. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the HSL to HEX

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Convert HSL color values to a HEX code.

HSL to HEX runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the HSL to HEX

Hue is a degree on the wheel, 0 and 360 both red. Saturation and lightness are percents. The HEX is the same colour in a form CSS accepts.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the HSL to HEX stops

HSL to HEX does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
