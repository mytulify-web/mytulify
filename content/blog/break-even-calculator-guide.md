[Break-Even Calculator](/freelancer-tools/break-even-calculator) is the tool for one job: find how many projects or hours you need each month to cover fixed costs. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Break-Even Calculator

Use the figures you and the client already discussed. A blank you leave in will be blank on the PDF.

Break-Even Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Break-Even Calculator

Break-even is the point where revenue covers fixed costs. In hours, it is fixed costs divided by the amount you keep from one hour. Below that, the month loses money.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.
