[Redirect Checker](/seo-web-tools/redirect-checker) is the tool for one job: follow a live URL and list every redirect hop, status code, and final destination. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Redirect Checker

Bring the URL, the HTML, or the keyword list the form asks for.

Redirect Checker runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Redirect Checker

Each hop has a status, usually 301 or 302, and a target. A chain longer than one hop wastes the crawl. The final URL should be the one you meant to publish.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the real page source or Search Console and confirm the change landed. A result on this page is not a ranking.

## Where the Redirect Checker stops

Redirect Checker does not control a search engine. It prepares or checks the input you gave it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
