[Find and Replace Text](/text-tools/find-and-replace-text) is the tool for one job: find and replace words or patterns in bulk text, with regex support. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Find and Replace Text

Paste the text you want changed. The box is the whole input.

Find and Replace Text runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Find and Replace Text

A plain replace changes every match. A regex replace treats dots and stars as patterns. Escape them if you mean a literal dot. Replace once on a copy and read a line you did not expect to change.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

For a transform, run it and spot one line. For a count, count one sentence yourself and compare.

## Where the Find and Replace Text stops

Find and Replace Text sees the text in the box. It does not open a file on your computer until you paste or upload.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
