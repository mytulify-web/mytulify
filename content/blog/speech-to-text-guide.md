[Speech to Text](/text-tools/speech-to-text) is the tool for one job: dictate speech into editable text in your browser with live transcript and multi-language support. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Speech to Text

Paste the text you want changed. The box is the whole input.

Speech to Text runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Speech to Text

The transcript follows the microphone. Names and jargon are the words it misses. Edit those. The audio is not stored as a recording unless the page says it is.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

For a transform, run it and spot one line. For a count, count one sentence yourself and compare.

## Where the Speech to Text stops

Speech to Text sees the text in the box. It does not open a file on your computer until you paste or upload.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
