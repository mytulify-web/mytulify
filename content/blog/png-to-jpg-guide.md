[PNG to JPG](/image-tools/png-to-jpg) is the tool for one job: convert PNG images to compressed JPG files. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the PNG to JPG

Start from the original PNG file, not a screenshot of it.

PNG to JPG runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the PNG to JPG

PNG transparency is lost. Soft edges and screenshots of text look worse after JPG compression. Set the quality high if the image contains text.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download at the size you will use. Check transparency, text, and the file size against the original.

## Where the PNG to JPG stops

PNG to JPG converts the file. It does not recover detail the source never had.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
