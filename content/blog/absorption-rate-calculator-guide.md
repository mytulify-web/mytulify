[Absorption Rate Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/absorption-rate-calculator) is the tool for one job: calculate real estate absorption rate and months of housing supply from sales and active listings. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Absorption Rate Calculator

Months of supply is the number of homes for sale divided by the number sold per month. Absorption rate is the share of the inventory that sold.

Absorption Rate Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Absorption Rate Calculator

If 120 homes are listed and 30 sell in a month, that is 4 months of supply, and the monthly absorption is 30/120 = 25%.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Double the sales and the months of supply should halve. The page is only as good as the sold count you typed.

## Where the Absorption Rate Calculator stops

One neighbourhood's sales do not describe a whole city. Use the same geography for listings and sales.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
