[Color Wheel](/color-tools/color-wheel) is the tool for one job: explore complementary and harmony colors on a wheel. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Color Wheel

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Explore complementary and harmony colors on a wheel.

Color Wheel runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Color Wheel

Explore complementary and harmony colors on a wheel. Copy the value into the place you will use it, because a picker on this page and a monitor elsewhere can disagree.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the Color Wheel stops

Color Wheel does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Color Wheel do?

It is there to explore complementary and harmony colors on a wheel. The form on the [Color Wheel](/color-tools/color-wheel) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Color Wheel result is usable?

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.
