## What to bring to the Shoe Size Converter

Pick the two units before you type the quantity. Convert shoe sizes between US, UK, EU, and centimeters.

Shoe Size Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Shoe Size Converter

US, UK, and EU sizes are charts, not a physical law. A men's US size and a women's US size with the same number are not the same length. Use the chart that matches the shoe.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Convert the result back the other way. You should land close to the number you started with. A large gap means the units are not the pair you think.

## Where the Shoe Size Converter stops

Shoe Size Converter converts units. It does not know which unit your drawing, recipe, or ticket required.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
