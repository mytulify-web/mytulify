[Triadic Color Generator](/color-tools/triadic-color-generator) is the tool for one job: create triadic three-color harmony schemes. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Triadic Color Generator

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Create triadic three-color harmony schemes.

Triadic Color Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Triadic Color Generator

A triad is three hues about 120 degrees apart. You should be able to see three distinct hues, not three shades of one.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the Triadic Color Generator stops

Triadic Color Generator does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
