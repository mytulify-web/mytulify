[Number to Words Converter](/unit-converters/number-to-words-converter) is the tool for one job: spell out numbers in words for checks and documents. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Number to Words Converter

Pick the two units before you type the quantity. Spell out numbers in words for checks and documents.

Number to Words Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Number to Words Converter

1042 in words is one thousand forty-two, without the word 'and' in the American style many cheques use, or with 'and' in other styles. Read which style the page printed before you put it on a cheque.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Convert the result back the other way. You should land close to the number you started with. A large gap means the units are not the pair you think.

## Where the Number to Words Converter stops

Number to Words Converter converts units. It does not know which unit your drawing, recipe, or ticket required.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
