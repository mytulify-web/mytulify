[Random Number Generator](/calculators/random-number-generator) is the tool for one job: generate random numbers within a custom range with options. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Random Number Generator

Set the lowest and highest number and how many results you want. Decide whether repeats are allowed.

Random Number Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Random Number Generator

A roll of one number from 1 to 6 should never show 0 or 7. Ask for twenty numbers and scan for anything outside the range.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

If you need a draw without duplicates, turn repeats off and do not ask for more numbers than the range contains.

## Where the Random Number Generator stops

Browser random numbers are fine for a game or a draw. They are not a cryptographic key.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
