[BMP to PDF](/image-tools/bmp-to-pdf) is the tool for one job: merge BMP images into a single PDF document. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the BMP to PDF

Start from the original BMP file, not a screenshot of it.

BMP to PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the BMP to PDF

Each BMP becomes a page or an image on a page. The PDF is a wrapper for sharing, not a smaller master of the bitmap.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download at the size you will use. Check transparency, text, and the file size against the original.

## Where the BMP to PDF stops

BMP to PDF converts the file. It does not recover detail the source never had.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
