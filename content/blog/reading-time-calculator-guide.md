[Reading Time Calculator](/text-tools/reading-time-calculator) is the tool for one job: estimate how long it takes to read any text based on words per minute. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Reading Time Calculator

Paste the text you want changed. The box is the whole input.

Reading Time Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Reading Time Calculator

Time is words divided by the words per minute you set. 250 words at 200 words per minute is a bit over a minute. Speaking time is slower than silent reading if the page offers both.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

For a transform, run it and spot one line. For a count, count one sentence yourself and compare.

## Where the Reading Time Calculator stops

Reading Time Calculator sees the text in the box. It does not open a file on your computer until you paste or upload.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
