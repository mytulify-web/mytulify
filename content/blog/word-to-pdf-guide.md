[Word to PDF](/pdf-tools/word-to-pdf) is the tool for one job: convert DOC/DOCX documents into PDF files. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Word to PDF

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Convert DOC/DOCX documents into PDF files.

Word to PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Word to PDF

The PDF is the fixed look of the document. Fonts that are not embedded can substitute. Open the PDF and check a heading and a page number.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Word to PDF stops

Word to PDF writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
