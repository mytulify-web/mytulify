[Title Case Converter](/text-tools/title-case-converter) is the tool for one job: apply proper title capitalization rules to headlines. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Title Case Converter

Paste the text you want changed. The box is the whole input.

Title Case Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Title Case Converter

Apply proper title capitalization rules to headlines. Paste a short sample whose answer you can check by eye, then paste the real text.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

For a transform, run it and spot one line. For a count, count one sentence yourself and compare.

## Where the Title Case Converter stops

Title Case Converter sees the text in the box. It does not open a file on your computer until you paste or upload.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
