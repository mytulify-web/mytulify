[Remove Line Breaks](/text-tools/remove-line-breaks) is the tool for one job: strip or merge line breaks and extra newlines from pasted text. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Remove Line Breaks

Paste the text you want changed. The box is the whole input.

Remove Line Breaks runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Remove Line Breaks

Strip or merge line breaks and extra newlines from pasted text. Paste a short sample whose answer you can check by eye, then paste the real text.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

For a transform, run it and spot one line. For a count, count one sentence yourself and compare.

## Where the Remove Line Breaks stops

Remove Line Breaks sees the text in the box. It does not open a file on your computer until you paste or upload.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
