[Lighten and Darken Color](/color-tools/lighten-darken-color) is the tool for one job: adjust a color's lightness up or down by percentage. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Lighten and Darken Color

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Adjust a color's lightness up or down by percentage.

Lighten and Darken Color runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Lighten and Darken Color

Adjust a color's lightness up or down by percentage. Copy the value into the place you will use it, because a picker on this page and a monitor elsewhere can disagree.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the Lighten and Darken Color stops

Lighten and Darken Color does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

It is there to adjust a color's lightness up or down by percentage. The form on the [Lighten and Darken Color](/color-tools/lighten-darken-color) page is the place you do that.
