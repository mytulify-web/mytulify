[Hashtag Counter](/social-media-tools/hashtag-counter) is the tool for one job: count hashtags in text and flag platform over-limits. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Hashtag Counter

Paste the caption, the size, or the mockup text you actually plan to publish.

Hashtag Counter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Hashtag Counter

Count the tags before you post. Instagram in particular gets noisy and can limit reach when a caption is a wall of tags. The number on the page is the count in the text you pasted.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Check the character count and the line breaks in the preview. Then paste into the network and look again. The network can still wrap differently.

## Where the Hashtag Counter stops

Hashtag Counter does not post for you. A mockup is a picture, not a live post.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
