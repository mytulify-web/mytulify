[Loan Comparison Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/loan-comparison-calculator) is the tool for one job: compare two loans side by side on monthly payment, total interest, and total amount paid. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Loan Comparison Calculator

Enter both loans: amount, rate, and term. Compare the monthly payment, the total interest, and the total paid.

Loan Comparison Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Loan Comparison Calculator

A lower rate wins on interest if the amount and the term match. A shorter term can have a higher payment and still cost less overall.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

If loan A and loan B have the same three inputs, the three results should match. That is the sanity check.

## Where the Loan Comparison Calculator stops

Points and fees belong in an APR comparison if you are looking at upfront cost, not only the note rate.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
