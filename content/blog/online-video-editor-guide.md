[Online Video Editor](/content-creator-tools/online-video-editor) is the tool for one job: edit videos in the browser — multi-clip timeline, trim/split/reverse, transitions, filters, FX, captions + SRT, logos, aspect ratios, speed & WebM export. Private on-device. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Online Video Editor

Bring the real source: the code, the job post, the diff, or the file.

Online Video Editor runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Online Video Editor

Trims, captions, and the export run in the browser. The export is a new file. Keep the original clips until you have watched the export.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Check names, numbers, and any line you would ship. Change one instruction and see whether the draft follows it.

## Where the Online Video Editor stops

This one runs in the browser. The file stays on your device.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
