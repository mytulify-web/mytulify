[Word Frequency Counter](/text-tools/word-frequency-counter) is the tool for one job: count how often each word appears and rank them by frequency. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Word Frequency Counter

Paste the text you want changed. The box is the whole input.

Word Frequency Counter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Word Frequency Counter

Count how often each word appears and rank them by frequency. Paste a short sample whose answer you can check by eye, then paste the real text.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

For a transform, run it and spot one line. For a count, count one sentence yourself and compare.

## Where the Word Frequency Counter stops

Word Frequency Counter sees the text in the box. It does not open a file on your computer until you paste or upload.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
