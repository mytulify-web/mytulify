[Marketplace Fee Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/marketplace-fee-calculator) is the tool for one job: calculate seller fees, shipping, product cost, net profit, and margin for an online marketplace listing. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Marketplace Fee Calculator

Net profit is the sale price minus the marketplace fee, shipping, and the product cost. Margin is that profit divided by the sale price.

Marketplace Fee Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Marketplace Fee Calculator

On a 40 sale with a 15% fee, the fee is 6. If shipping is 5 and the item cost is 18, profit is 40 − 6 − 5 − 18 = 11, and the margin is 11/40 = 27.5%.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

The fee should be the percent times the price the form says the percent applies to. Some marketplaces charge the percent on price plus shipping. Read that label.

## Where the Marketplace Fee Calculator stops

Returns, ads, and a payment hold are not in the profit until you type them as costs.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
