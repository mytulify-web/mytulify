[RGB to HSL](/color-tools/rgb-to-hsl) is the tool for one job: convert RGB color values to HSL. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the RGB to HSL

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Convert RGB color values to HSL.

RGB to HSL runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the RGB to HSL

Changing lightness in HSL is easier than guessing RGB. Convert, move lightness, and convert back if you need the RGB again.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the RGB to HSL stops

RGB to HSL does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
