[Vehicle Depreciation Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/vehicle-depreciation-calculator) is the tool for one job: project car value year by year with a declining-balance depreciation rate. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Vehicle Depreciation Calculator

Declining balance takes a percent off the current value each year, not off the original price.

Vehicle Depreciation Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Vehicle Depreciation Calculator

A 30,000 car losing 15% a year is worth 25,500 after one year and 25,500 × 0.85 after two years, which is 21,675. A flat 15% of 30,000 each year would be a different, lower path.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Year 0 should be the price you typed. Year 1 should be that price times one minus the rate.

## Where the Vehicle Depreciation Calculator stops

A real resale price depends on miles, condition, and the market. The percent is your assumption.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
