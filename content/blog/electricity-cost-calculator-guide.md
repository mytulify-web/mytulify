[Electricity Cost Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/electricity-cost-calculator) is the tool for one job: estimate appliance electricity cost from watts, hours per day, and your price per kWh. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Electricity Cost Calculator

Cost is watts turned into kilowatts, times hours, times the price per kWh. A 1000 watt load for 1 hour is 1 kWh.

Electricity Cost Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Electricity Cost Calculator

A 100 watt bulb for 5 hours a day uses 0.5 kWh a day. At 0.20 per kWh that is 0.10 a day, about 3 a month at 30 days.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

The monthly figure should be about 30 times the daily figure. If you entered hours per month in the per-day box, the cost is 30 times too high.

## Where the Electricity Cost Calculator stops

Standby draw and a utility's tiered rates are not included unless you fold them into the price you type.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
