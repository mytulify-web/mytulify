[PNG to SVG](/image-tools/png-to-svg) is the tool for one job: trace a raster PNG into a scalable SVG vector. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the PNG to SVG

Start from the original PNG file, not a screenshot of it.

PNG to SVG runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the PNG to SVG

Tracing a photo into vectors guesses shapes. Logos with flat colours trace cleanly. A portrait will not become a crisp illustration.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download at the size you will use. Check transparency, text, and the file size against the original.

## Where the PNG to SVG stops

PNG to SVG converts the file. It does not recover detail the source never had.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
