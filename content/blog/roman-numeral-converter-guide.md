[Roman Numeral Converter](/unit-converters/roman-numeral-converter) is the tool for one job: convert numbers to Roman numerals and back. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Roman Numeral Converter

Pick the two units before you type the quantity. Convert numbers to Roman numerals and back.

Roman Numeral Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Roman Numeral Converter

1994 is MCMXCIV: 1000 + 900 + 90 + 4. A smaller numeral before a larger one subtracts. IIII is not the standard form. IV is.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Convert the result back the other way. You should land close to the number you started with. A large gap means the units are not the pair you think.

## Where the Roman Numeral Converter stops

Roman Numeral Converter converts units. It does not know which unit your drawing, recipe, or ticket required.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
