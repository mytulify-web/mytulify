[Pixelate Image](/image-tools/pixelate-image) is the tool for one job: pixelate or censor regions of an image. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Pixelate Image

Use the original image. Pixelate or censor regions of an image.

Pixelate Image runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Pixelate Image

Pixelate replaces a region with big blocks so a face, a plate, or a message cannot be read. The rest of the photo stays sharp. You cannot un-pixelate the download.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the Pixelate Image stops

Pixelate Image changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Pixelate Image do?

It is there to pixelate or censor regions of an image. The form on the [Pixelate Image](/image-tools/pixelate-image) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Pixelate Image result is usable?

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

### What should I not trust the Pixelate Image to decide?

Pixelate Image changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.
