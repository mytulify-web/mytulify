[Collage Maker](/image-tools/collage-maker) is the tool for one job: combine multiple photos into a grid collage. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Collage Maker

Use the original image. Combine multiple photos into a grid collage.

Collage Maker runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Collage Maker

The grid sets the aspect of each cell. Photos are cropped to the cell. Check faces near the edges before you download.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the Collage Maker stops

Collage Maker changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Collage Maker do?

It is there to combine multiple photos into a grid collage. The form on the [Collage Maker](/image-tools/collage-maker) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Collage Maker result is usable?

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

### What should I not trust the Collage Maker to decide?

Collage Maker changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.
