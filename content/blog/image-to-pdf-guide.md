[Image to PDF](/image-tools/image-to-pdf) is the tool for one job: convert images into a single downloadable PDF. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Image to PDF

Use the original image. Convert images into a single downloadable PDF.

Image to PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Image to PDF

The PDF wraps the image on a page. It does not OCR the words in the picture. The page size follows the image unless you pick a paper size.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the Image to PDF stops

Image to PDF changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Image to PDF do?

It is there to convert images into a single downloadable PDF. The form on the [Image to PDF](/image-tools/image-to-pdf) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Image to PDF result is usable?

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

### What should I not trust the Image to PDF to decide?
