[Instagram Caption Generator](/social-media-tools/instagram-caption-generator) is the tool for one job: generate caption ideas from a topic plus hashtags and emojis. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Instagram Caption Generator

Paste the caption, the size, or the mockup text you actually plan to publish.

Instagram Caption Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Instagram Caption Generator

Generate caption ideas from a topic plus hashtags and emojis. The preview matches the text you typed. Read it once as a stranger would.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Check the character count and the line breaks in the preview. Then paste into the network and look again. The network can still wrap differently.

## Where the Instagram Caption Generator stops

Instagram Caption Generator does not post for you. A mockup is a picture, not a live post.
