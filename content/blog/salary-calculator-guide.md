[Salary Calculator](/calculators/salary-calculator) is the tool for one job: convert pay between hourly, weekly, monthly, and annual rates. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Salary Calculator

Enter one rate and the schedule you want to convert. Hourly to annual assumes a number of hours per week, often 40.

Salary Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Salary Calculator

20 per hour at 40 hours and 52 weeks is 41,600 a year, before tax. A 37.5-hour week is 39,000. The hours assumption is part of the answer.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Divide the annual figure back by 52 and by the weekly hours. You should land on the hourly rate you started with.

## Where the Salary Calculator stops

It converts rates. It does not compute tax, overtime law, or unpaid leave.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
