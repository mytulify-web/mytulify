[Reverse PDF Pages](/pdf-tools/reverse-pdf-pages) is the tool for one job: flip the page order of a PDF document. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Reverse PDF Pages

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Flip the page order of a PDF document.

Reverse PDF Pages runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Reverse PDF Pages

The last page becomes the first. A document that was pages 1 to 10 reads 10 to 1. This is useful for a stack that was scanned backwards.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Reverse PDF Pages stops

Reverse PDF Pages writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
