[Ohm's Law Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/ohms-law-calculator) is the tool for one job: solve voltage, current, resistance, and watts with Ohm's law from any two electrical values. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Ohm's Law Calculator

Ohm's law is V = I × R. Watts are V × I. Give the page any two of voltage, current, and resistance and it solves the others.

Ohm's Law Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Ohm's Law Calculator

A 12 volt supply across 6 ohms draws 2 amps and uses 24 watts. If you instead enter 12 volts and 2 amps, the resistance should come back as 6 ohms.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Check that volts equal amps times ohms on the numbers the page shows. All four figures should describe the same circuit.

## Where the Ohm's Law Calculator stops

This is DC ohm's law for a resistive load. Motors, capacitors, and power factors are a different page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
