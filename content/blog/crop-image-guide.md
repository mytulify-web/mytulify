[Crop Image](/image-tools/crop-image) is the tool for one job: crop images freely or to fixed aspect ratios. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Crop Image

Use the original image. Crop images freely or to fixed aspect ratios.

Crop Image runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Crop Image

The crop throws away pixels outside the rectangle. A fixed aspect ratio such as 1:1 will cut whichever side does not fit. Nothing outside the crop can be restored from the download.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the Crop Image stops

Crop Image changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Crop Image do?

It is there to crop images freely or to fixed aspect ratios. The form on the [Crop Image](/image-tools/crop-image) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Crop Image result is usable?

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

### What should I not trust the Crop Image to decide?

Crop Image changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.
