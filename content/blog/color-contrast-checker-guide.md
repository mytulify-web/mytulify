[Color Contrast Checker](/color-tools/color-contrast-checker) is the tool for one job: check WCAG contrast ratio between two colors. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Color Contrast Checker

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Check WCAG contrast ratio between two colors.

Color Contrast Checker runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Color Contrast Checker

Check the exact pair you will ship, not a nearby shade.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the Color Contrast Checker stops

Color Contrast Checker does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Color Contrast Checker do?

It is there to check WCAG contrast ratio between two colors. The form on the [Color Contrast Checker](/color-tools/color-contrast-checker) page is the place you do that.
