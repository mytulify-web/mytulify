[Fuel Mix Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/fuel-mix-calculator) is the tool for one job: calculate 2-stroke oil for a 50:1, 40:1, 32:1, or 25:1 gas mix in ounces and milliliters. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Fuel Mix Calculator

A 50:1 mix is 50 parts gasoline to 1 part oil. Oil in ounces is the gallons of gas times 128, divided by the ratio.

Fuel Mix Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Fuel Mix Calculator

One US gallon at 50:1 needs 128/50 = 2.56 ounces of oil, about 76 ml. At 40:1 the same gallon needs more oil, 3.2 ounces.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

A smaller ratio number means richer oil. 25:1 should call for about twice the oil of 50:1.

## Where the Fuel Mix Calculator stops

Use the ratio printed on the engine. A chainsaw and a boat motor do not share a guess.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
