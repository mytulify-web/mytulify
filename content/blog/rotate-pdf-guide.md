[Rotate PDF](/pdf-tools/rotate-pdf) is the tool for one job: rotate one or all pages of a PDF and save permanently. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Rotate PDF

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Rotate one or all pages of a PDF and save permanently.

Rotate PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Rotate PDF

Rotation is saved into the file. A viewer that ignored a display-only rotate will now show the page turned. Check every page you marked, not only the first.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Rotate PDF stops

Rotate PDF writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
