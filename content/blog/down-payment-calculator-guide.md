[Down Payment Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/down-payment-calculator) is the tool for one job: compare 3%, 5%, 10%, 20%, and a custom down payment, with loan amount and whether PMI is likely. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Down Payment Calculator

The loan amount is the price minus the down payment. Compare 3%, 5%, 10%, 20%, and your own percent.

Down Payment Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Down Payment Calculator

Below 20% down, lenders often charge PMI. The page flags that likelihood. It is not a quote for the PMI premium.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

20% of the price should produce a loan of 80% and clear the PMI flag. 10% should keep the flag.

## Where the Down Payment Calculator stops

Assistance programs and a seller credit can change the cash you bring. They are not in the percent buttons.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
