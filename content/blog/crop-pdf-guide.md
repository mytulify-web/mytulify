[Crop PDF](/pdf-tools/crop-pdf) is the tool for one job: trim margins and crop the visible area of PDF pages. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Crop PDF

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Trim margins and crop the visible area of PDF pages.

Crop PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Crop PDF

Cropping hides the margin in the page box. It does not always delete the hidden content from the file. Do not use crop to remove a secret. Delete or redact for that.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Crop PDF stops

Crop PDF writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

It is there to trim margins and crop the visible area of PDF pages. The form on the [Crop PDF](/pdf-tools/crop-pdf) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Crop PDF result is usable?

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

### What should I not trust the Crop PDF to decide?
