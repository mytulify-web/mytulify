[Home Color Visualizer](/image-tools/home-color-visualizer) is the tool for one job: upload a photo of your home or room, try wall/door/furniture colors, and preview what looks best. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Home Color Visualizer

Use the original image. Upload a photo of your home or room, try wall/door/furniture colors, and preview what looks best.

Home Color Visualizer runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Home Color Visualizer

You paint regions of a photo, not the room itself. Lighting in the photo changes the colour you see. Compare two colours on the same wall before you buy paint.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the Home Color Visualizer stops

Home Color Visualizer changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
