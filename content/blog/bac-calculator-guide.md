[Blood Alcohol (BAC) Calculator](/health-tools/bac-calculator) is the tool for one job: estimate blood alcohol concentration with the Widmark formula and a time-to-sober estimate. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Blood Alcohol (BAC) Calculator

The Widmark estimate uses alcohol consumed, body weight, and a sex-based factor for body water, then subtracts alcohol burned over time.

Blood Alcohol (BAC) Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Blood Alcohol (BAC) Calculator

More drinks and less body weight raise the estimate. Time since the first drink lowers it, often by about 0.015 BAC per hour in the simple model, which is an average, not your liver.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

At zero drinks the result should be zero. One drink just consumed should not already be zero.

## Where the Blood Alcohol (BAC) Calculator stops

This is not a legal measurement. A breath or blood test is. Do not use the number to decide whether to drive.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
