[Bank Statement to Excel](/pdf-tools/bank-statement-to-excel) is the tool for one job: extract bank statement PDF data to CSV for Excel. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Bank Statement to Excel

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Extract bank statement PDF data to CSV for Excel.

Bank Statement to Excel runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Bank Statement to Excel

The CSV is only as good as the text in the statement. A scanned statement has no text to extract. Check a known transaction, the date, and the amount sign.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Bank Statement to Excel stops

Bank Statement to Excel writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
