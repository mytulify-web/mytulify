[Grayscale Image](/image-tools/grayscale-image) is the tool for one job: convert a color image to black and white. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Grayscale Image

Use the original image. Convert a color image to black and white.

Grayscale Image runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Grayscale Image

Grayscale keeps the brightness and drops the hue. A red and a green of the same brightness become the same gray. The download is not a colour file with a filter you can undo.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the Grayscale Image stops

Grayscale Image changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

It is there to convert a color image to black and white. The form on the [Grayscale Image](/image-tools/grayscale-image) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Grayscale Image result is usable?

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

### What should I not trust the Grayscale Image to decide?
