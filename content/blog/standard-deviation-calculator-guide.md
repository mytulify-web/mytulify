[Standard Deviation Calculator](/calculators/standard-deviation-calculator) is the tool for one job: compute mean, variance, and standard deviation for a data set. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Standard Deviation Calculator

Paste the data set. Know whether you want the sample standard deviation, which divides by n−1, or the population figure, which divides by n.

Standard Deviation Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Standard Deviation Calculator

The numbers 2, 4, and 6 have a mean of 4. The sample standard deviation is larger than the population one because the divisor is 2 instead of 3.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Check the mean by hand. If the mean is wrong, the spread is wrong. Then confirm which divisor the page used.

## Where the Standard Deviation Calculator stops

It describes the numbers you typed. It does not know if one of them was a typo or a different unit.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
