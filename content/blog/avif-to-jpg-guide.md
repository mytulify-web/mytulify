[AVIF to JPG](/image-tools/avif-to-jpg) is the tool for one job: convert modern AVIF images to widely supported JPG. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the AVIF to JPG

Start from the original AVIF file, not a screenshot of it.

AVIF to JPG runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the AVIF to JPG

AVIF is a newer photo format some older apps cannot open. JPG is the compatible copy. Transparency, if the AVIF had it, becomes a background.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download at the size you will use. Check transparency, text, and the file size against the original.

## Where the AVIF to JPG stops

AVIF to JPG converts the file. It does not recover detail the source never had.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
