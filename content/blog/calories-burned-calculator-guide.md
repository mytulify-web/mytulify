[Calories Burned Calculator](/health-tools/calories-burned-calculator) is the tool for one job: estimate calories burned for dozens of activities using MET values, your weight and duration. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Calories Burned Calculator

MET values estimate how hard an activity is. Calories are about MET times body weight in kilograms times hours.

Calories Burned Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Calories Burned Calculator

Walking at a moderate MET, a heavier person burns more than a lighter person for the same hour. Doubling the minutes should double the calories.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare two activities at the same weight and duration. The one with the higher MET should burn more.

## Where the Calories Burned Calculator stops

A fitness tracker that sees your heart rate is a different measurement. MET tables are averages.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
