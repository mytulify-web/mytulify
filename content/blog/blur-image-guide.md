[Blur Image](/image-tools/blur-image) is the tool for one job: apply adjustable blur to all or part of an image. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Blur Image

Use the original image. Apply adjustable blur to all or part of an image.

Blur Image runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Blur Image

Blur mixes neighbouring pixels. A small radius softens. A large radius hides detail. If you blur only a region, the edge of that region should be where you set it.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the Blur Image stops

Blur Image changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Blur Image do?

It is there to apply adjustable blur to all or part of an image. The form on the [Blur Image](/image-tools/blur-image) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Blur Image result is usable?

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

### What should I not trust the Blur Image to decide?

Blur Image changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.
