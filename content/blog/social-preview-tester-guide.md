[Social Preview Tester](/social-media-tools/social-preview-tester) is the tool for one job: preview how a link will look when shared across platforms. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Social Preview Tester

Paste the caption, the size, or the mockup text you actually plan to publish.

Social Preview Tester runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Social Preview Tester

Preview how a link will look when shared across platforms. The preview matches the text you typed. Read it once as a stranger would.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Check the character count and the line breaks in the preview. Then paste into the network and look again. The network can still wrap differently.

## Where the Social Preview Tester stops

Social Preview Tester does not post for you. A mockup is a picture, not a live post.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
