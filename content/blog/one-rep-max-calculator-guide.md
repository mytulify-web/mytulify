[One Rep Max Calculator](/health-tools/one-rep-max-calculator) is the tool for one job: estimate your 1RM from a lift and reps, with a full percentage-of-max training table. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the One Rep Max Calculator

A 1RM estimate predicts the heaviest single lift from a lighter set. The Epley idea is weight times (1 + reps / 30). Other formulas differ a little.

One Rep Max Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the One Rep Max Calculator

100 kg for 5 reps is about 100 × (1 + 5/30) = 117 kg under Epley. The training table then shows percents of that 117, such as 85% for heavier sets.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

One rep at a weight should return that same weight as the max. If it does not, the formula is being applied to a rep you did not intend.

## Where the One Rep Max Calculator stops

Estimates get worse above about 10 reps. This does not know your form or an injury.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
