[GIF Optimizer](/image-tools/gif-optimizer) is the tool for one job: compress and optimize GIF file size in your browser. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the GIF Optimizer

Use the original image. Compress and optimize GIF file size in your browser.

GIF Optimizer runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the GIF Optimizer

Optimization drops extra colours or frames to shrink the file. Look at gradients and text. Those break first.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the GIF Optimizer stops

GIF Optimizer changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
