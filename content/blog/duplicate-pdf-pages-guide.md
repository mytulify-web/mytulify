[Duplicate PDF Pages](/pdf-tools/duplicate-pdf-pages) is the tool for one job: copy and repeat selected pages within a PDF. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Duplicate PDF Pages

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Copy and repeat selected pages within a PDF.

Duplicate PDF Pages runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Duplicate PDF Pages

The pages you pick are repeated in the file. The page count rises. Confirm you duplicated the page you meant, not the one next to it.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Duplicate PDF Pages stops

Duplicate PDF Pages writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
