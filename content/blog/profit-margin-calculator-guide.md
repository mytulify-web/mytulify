[Profit Margin Calculator](/calculators/profit-margin-calculator) is the tool for one job: calculate profit, profit margin %, and markup from cost and selling price. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Profit Margin Calculator

Margin and markup are not the same. Margin is profit divided by the selling price. Markup is profit divided by the cost.

Profit Margin Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Profit Margin Calculator

Cost 60, price 100: profit is 40. Margin is 40/100 = 40%. Markup is 40/60 = 66.7%. A 40% markup on 60 is a price of 84, not 100.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Read the label on the percent. If you wanted margin and the page shows markup, you will underprice.

## Where the Profit Margin Calculator stops

It ignores overhead you did not type: rent, tax, shipping, and payment fees.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
