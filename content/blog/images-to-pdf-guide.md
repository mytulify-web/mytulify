[Images to PDF](/pdf-tools/images-to-pdf) is the tool for one job: batch combine multiple images into one ordered PDF. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Images to PDF

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Batch combine multiple images into one ordered PDF.

Images to PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Images to PDF

The PDF page order is the image order you set. Mixed orientations need a look at each page, because a portrait phone photo on a landscape page leaves large margins.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Images to PDF stops

Images to PDF writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
