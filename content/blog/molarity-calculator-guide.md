[Molarity Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/molarity-calculator) is the tool for one job: calculate solution molarity from grams and molar mass, or the grams to weigh for a target molarity. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Molarity Calculator

Molarity is moles per litre. Moles are grams divided by the molar mass in grams per mole.

Molarity Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Molarity Calculator

58.44 grams of NaCl is one mole. Dissolved in 1 litre, that is a 1 molar solution. To make 0.5 litres of 0.5 M, you need 0.25 moles, about 14.6 grams.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

The grams the page asks you to weigh should scale with both the volume and the molarity. Double either one and the grams should double.

## Where the Molarity Calculator stops

The molar mass has to be the substance you are weighing. A hydrate and an anhydrous salt are different masses.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
