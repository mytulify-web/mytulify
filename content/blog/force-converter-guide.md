## What to bring to the Force Converter

Pick the two units before you type the quantity. Convert newtons, pounds-force, kilogram-force, and dynes.

Force Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Force Converter

Convert newtons, pounds-force, kilogram-force, and dynes. Convert a value you already know in both units, such as a round 1 or 10, and see that the other side matches the definition you trust. Then convert the measurement you actually have.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Convert the result back the other way. You should land close to the number you started with. A large gap means the units are not the pair you think.

## Where the Force Converter stops

Force Converter converts units. It does not know which unit your drawing, recipe, or ticket required.
