[Favicon Generator](/image-tools/favicon-generator) is the tool for one job: create multi-size favicons and ICO files from an image. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Favicon Generator

Use the original image. Create multi-size favicons and ICO files from an image.

Favicon Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Favicon Generator

A favicon is the tiny icon in a browser tab. It needs to be simple at 16 and 32 pixels. The page builds those sizes and an ICO from the image you upload.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the Favicon Generator stops

Favicon Generator changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
