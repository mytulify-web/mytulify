[GIF to Video](/image-tools/gif-to-video) is the tool for one job: convert animated GIFs to WebM video. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the GIF to Video

Start from the original GIF file, not a screenshot of it.

GIF to Video runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the GIF to Video

The animation becomes a WebM video. Video does not use GIF's 256-colour limit in the same way, so the motion can look cleaner, but a very old device may not play WebM.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download at the size you will use. Check transparency, text, and the file size against the original.

## Where the GIF to Video stops

GIF to Video converts the file. It does not recover detail the source never had.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
