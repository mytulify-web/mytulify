[Hex to RGB Converter](/developer-tools/hex-to-rgb-converter) is the tool for one job: convert colors between HEX, RGB and HSL formats. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Hex to RGB Converter

Paste a real snippet, not a description of the snippet, unless the tool asks you to describe one.

Hex to RGB Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Hex to RGB Converter

Convert colors between HEX, RGB and HSL formats. Feed it a small valid sample and a sample you know is broken. The valid one should succeed and the broken one should point at the problem.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Diff the output against the input for a formatter. For a generator, run the result in the real tool that will consume it.

## Where the Hex to RGB Converter stops

Hex to RGB Converter does not see your repository, your server, or the version of the library on your machine.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
