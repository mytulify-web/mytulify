[GIF Trimmer](/image-tools/gif-trimmer) is the tool for one job: trim GIF animations to a specific frame range. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the GIF Trimmer

Use the original image. Trim GIF animations to a specific frame range.

GIF Trimmer runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the GIF Trimmer

Trimming keeps a start frame and an end frame and drops the rest. The timing of the frames you keep should stay as it was.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the GIF Trimmer stops

GIF Trimmer changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the GIF Trimmer do?

It is there to trim GIF animations to a specific frame range. The form on the [GIF Trimmer](/image-tools/gif-trimmer) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the GIF Trimmer result is usable?

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

### What should I not trust the GIF Trimmer to decide?

GIF Trimmer changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.
