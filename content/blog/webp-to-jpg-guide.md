[WebP to JPG](/image-tools/webp-to-jpg) is the tool for one job: convert WebP images to widely supported JPG. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the WebP to JPG

Start from the original WEBP file, not a screenshot of it.

WebP to JPG runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the WebP to JPG

JPG has no transparency. Any clear pixels in the WebP become a solid background. The file gets smaller because JPG throws away detail.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download at the size you will use. Check transparency, text, and the file size against the original.

## Where the WebP to JPG stops

WebP to JPG converts the file. It does not recover detail the source never had.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
