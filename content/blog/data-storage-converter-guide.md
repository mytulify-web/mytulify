[Data Storage Converter](/unit-converters/data-storage-converter) is the tool for one job: convert bytes, kilobytes, megabytes, gigabytes, and terabytes. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Data Storage Converter

Pick the two units before you type the quantity. Convert bytes, kilobytes, megabytes, gigabytes, and terabytes.

Data Storage Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Data Storage Converter

In the decimal SI scale, 1 kilobyte is 1000 bytes and 1 megabyte is 1000 kilobytes. Some software uses 1024. The page should say which scale it used. A drive sold as 1 TB holds about 10^12 bytes, which an OS may show as less.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Convert the result back the other way. You should land close to the number you started with. A large gap means the units are not the pair you think.

## Where the Data Storage Converter stops

Data Storage Converter converts units. It does not know which unit your drawing, recipe, or ticket required.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
