[Readability Score Checker](/seo-web-tools/readability-checker) is the tool for one job: compute Flesch reading ease and grade-level scores for text. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Readability Score Checker

Bring the URL, the HTML, or the keyword list the form asks for.

Readability Score Checker runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Readability Score Checker

Flesch reading ease scores shorter sentences and shorter words as easier. It does not know whether the facts are right. A simple sentence can still be false.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the real page source or Search Console and confirm the change landed. A result on this page is not a ranking.

## Where the Readability Score Checker stops

Readability Score Checker does not control a search engine. It prepares or checks the input you gave it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
