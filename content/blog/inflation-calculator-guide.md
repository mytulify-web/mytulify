[Inflation Calculator](/calculators/inflation-calculator) is the tool for one job: see what today’s money will be worth later — or was worth in the past — using a custom inflation rate. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Inflation Calculator

Enter an amount, a start, an end, and an inflation rate. The page compounds that rate. It does not download a national price index unless it says so.

Inflation Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Inflation Calculator

At 6% a year, 100 today is about 100 × 1.06³ after three years, which is about 119. The same math backward tells you what 100 in the future is in today's money.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

A higher rate and a longer gap should both raise the future amount. If one of them does not, the fields are swapped.

## Where the Inflation Calculator stops

Your personal costs can rise faster or slower than the single rate you typed.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
