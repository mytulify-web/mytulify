## What to bring to the Freelance Proposal Generator

Use the figures you and the client already discussed. A blank you leave in will be blank on the PDF.

## A worked pass with the Freelance Proposal Generator

Build a client proposal with summary, scope, timeline, pricing, and about section — export PDF. Put in the numbers and the names from the real job, not a sample, before you export.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Read the export from top to bottom, including the total and the dates. Then send it.

Freelance Proposal Generator drafts the document or the number. It is not your lawyer, your accountant, or the client's acceptance.
