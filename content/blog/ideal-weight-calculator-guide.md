[Ideal Weight Calculator](/calculators/ideal-weight-calculator) is the tool for one job: suggest a healthy weight range based on height, age, and gender. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Ideal Weight Calculator

Enter height, and age or sex if the form asks. Different formulas, such as Devine or Hamwi, do not return the same weight.

Ideal Weight Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Ideal Weight Calculator

Read the range, not a single target. A healthy range is wider than one 'ideal' kilo.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

If two formulas are shown, the gap between them is the uncertainty. Do not treat the lower number as a goal by itself.

## Where the Ideal Weight Calculator stops

It does not know muscle, frame, or a clinician's target.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
