[Percentage Calculator](/calculators/percentage-calculator) is the tool for one job: find percentages, percentage change, and what percent one number is of another. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Percentage Calculator

Know which question you are asking: what is X percent of Y, what percent X is of Y, or the percent change from A to B.

Percentage Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Percentage Calculator

15% of 80 is 12. From 80 to 92 is a rise of 12, which is 12 / 80 = 15%. Those are different buttons, not the same sum.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Say the question out loud before you read the answer. A percent change uses the starting number as the base.

## Where the Percentage Calculator stops

It does not know which number is the base unless you put it in the field that asks for the base.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
