[Hull Speed Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/hull-speed-calculator) is the tool for one job: calculate displacement hull speed in knots, mph, and km/h from waterline length. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Hull Speed Calculator

Displacement hull speed in knots is about 1.34 times the square root of the waterline length in feet.

Hull Speed Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Hull Speed Calculator

A boat with a 25 ft waterline has a hull speed near 1.34 × 5 = 6.7 knots. In mph that is 6.7 × 1.151, about 7.7 mph.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

A longer waterline should raise the speed with the square root, not in a straight line. Quadrupling the length doubles the hull speed.

## Where the Hull Speed Calculator stops

Planing hulls and catamarans are not limited by this formula. It describes a displacement hull.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
