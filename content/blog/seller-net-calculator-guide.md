[Seller Net Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/seller-net-calculator) is the tool for one job: estimate seller net proceeds after commission, mortgage payoff, closing costs, and repairs. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Seller Net Calculator

Net to the seller is the sale price minus commission, the mortgage payoff, closing costs, and repairs.

Seller Net Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Seller Net Calculator

A 400,000 sale with a 6% commission is 24,000 off before the loan payoff. A payoff of 250,000 and 8,000 of costs leaves 118,000, before any repair you still have to do.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

The commission should move when you change the percent, and it should be the percent times the price, not times the equity.

## Where the Seller Net Calculator stops

Prorated taxes and a buyer credit can change the closing statement. Add them if you already know them.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
