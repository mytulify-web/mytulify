[Air Changes Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/air-changes-calculator) is the tool for one job: find air changes per hour from room cubic feet and fan CFM, plus minutes for one full air change. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Air Changes Calculator

Enter the room's cubic feet and the fan's CFM. Air changes per hour are CFM times 60, divided by the volume.

Air Changes Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Air Changes Calculator

An 80 CFM fan in an 800 cubic foot room gives 6 air changes an hour. One full change takes 10 minutes.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

If you type the room in inches, the volume is huge and the air changes collapse toward zero. Use feet.

## Where the Air Changes Calculator stops

It does not measure the fan. It uses the CFM you claim the fan moves.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
