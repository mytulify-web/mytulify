[PX to REM Converter](/developer-tools/px-to-rem-converter) is the tool for one job: convert CSS pixels to rem (and back) with a customizable root font size. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the PX to REM Converter

Paste a real snippet, not a description of the snippet, unless the tool asks you to describe one.

PX to REM Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the PX to REM Converter

rem is the root font size. At a 16 px root, 16 px is 1 rem and 24 px is 1.5 rem. Change the root and every rem value moves.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Diff the output against the input for a formatter. For a generator, run the result in the real tool that will consume it.

## Where the PX to REM Converter stops

PX to REM Converter does not see your repository, your server, or the version of the library on your machine.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
