[Schema Markup Generator](/seo-web-tools/schema-markup-generator) is the tool for one job: generate JSON-LD structured data for articles, products, FAQs, and more. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Schema Markup Generator

Bring the URL, the HTML, or the keyword list the form asks for.

Schema Markup Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Schema Markup Generator

JSON-LD is a script tag with a type such as Article or FAQPage. The required fields depend on the type. Validate it before you ship it. Markup does not create a review you did not write.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the real page source or Search Console and confirm the change landed. A result on this page is not a ranking.

## Where the Schema Markup Generator stops

Schema Markup Generator does not control a search engine. It prepares or checks the input you gave it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
