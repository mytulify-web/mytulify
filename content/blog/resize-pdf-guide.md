[Resize PDF](/pdf-tools/resize-pdf) is the tool for one job: scale PDF pages to A4, Letter, or custom dimensions. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Resize PDF

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Scale PDF pages to A4, Letter, or custom dimensions.

Resize PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Resize PDF

Scaling to A4 or Letter changes the page box. Content may shrink to fit or be clipped. Look at a page with a footer. Footers are the first thing cut off.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Resize PDF stops

Resize PDF writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

It is there to scale PDF pages to A4, Letter, or custom dimensions. The form on the [Resize PDF](/pdf-tools/resize-pdf) page is the place you do that.
