[Meme Generator](/image-tools/meme-generator) is the tool for one job: add top and bottom text captions to an image. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Meme Generator

Use the original image. Add top and bottom text captions to an image.

Meme Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Meme Generator

Top and bottom captions sit in the margin of the picture. Long text wraps or overflows. Download and read the caption at the size you will post.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the Meme Generator stops

Meme Generator changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Meme Generator do?

It is there to add top and bottom text captions to an image. The form on the [Meme Generator](/image-tools/meme-generator) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Meme Generator result is usable?

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

### What should I not trust the Meme Generator to decide?

Meme Generator changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.
