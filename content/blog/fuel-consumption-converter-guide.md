[Fuel Consumption Converter](/unit-converters/fuel-consumption-converter) is the tool for one job: convert MPG, km/L, and L/100km fuel economy units. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Fuel Consumption Converter

Pick the two units before you type the quantity. Convert MPG, km/L, and L/100km fuel economy units.

Fuel Consumption Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Fuel Consumption Converter

MPG and L/100 km run in opposite directions. Lower L/100 km is better economy. You cannot add them. US gallons and UK gallons make two different MPG numbers.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Convert the result back the other way. You should land close to the number you started with. A large gap means the units are not the pair you think.

## Where the Fuel Consumption Converter stops

Fuel Consumption Converter converts units. It does not know which unit your drawing, recipe, or ticket required.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
