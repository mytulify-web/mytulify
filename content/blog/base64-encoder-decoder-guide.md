[Base64 Encoder/Decoder](/developer-tools/base64-encoder-decoder) is the tool for one job: encode and decode text and files to and from Base64. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Base64 Encoder/Decoder

Paste a real snippet, not a description of the snippet, unless the tool asks you to describe one.

Base64 Encoder/Decoder runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Base64 Encoder/Decoder

Base64 is a way to write bytes as text. Encoding 'hi' is not encryption. Decoding fails when the string was wrapped or lost its padding.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Diff the output against the input for a formatter. For a generator, run the result in the real tool that will consume it.

## Where the Base64 Encoder/Decoder stops

Base64 Encoder/Decoder does not see your repository, your server, or the version of the library on your machine.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
