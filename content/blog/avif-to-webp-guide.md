[AVIF to WebP](/image-tools/avif-to-webp) is the tool for one job: convert AVIF images to efficient WebP format. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the AVIF to WebP

Start from the original AVIF file, not a screenshot of it.

AVIF to WebP runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the AVIF to WebP

Both are modern photo formats. WebP is the one to pick when the destination accepts WebP but not AVIF. Compare file size at the same visual size.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download at the size you will use. Check transparency, text, and the file size against the original.

## Where the AVIF to WebP stops

AVIF to WebP converts the file. It does not recover detail the source never had.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
