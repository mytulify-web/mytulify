[HEIC to JPG](/image-tools/heic-to-jpg) is the tool for one job: convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG images. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the HEIC to JPG

Start from the original HEIC file, not a screenshot of it.

HEIC to JPG runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the HEIC to JPG

HEIC is the iPhone photo format. The JPG is the shareable copy. Orientation can be stored sideways in the file. The preview should look upright.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download at the size you will use. Check transparency, text, and the file size against the original.

## Where the HEIC to JPG stops

HEIC to JPG converts the file. It does not recover detail the source never had.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
