[Due Date Calculator](/calculators/due-date-calculator) is the tool for one job: estimate a pregnancy due date from the last menstrual period. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Due Date Calculator

Use the first day of the last menstrual period and the cycle length the form assumes, often 28 days.

Due Date Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Due Date Calculator

Naegele's rule adds 280 days, which is 40 weeks, to that last-period date when the cycle is 28 days. A longer cycle shifts the date later.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Count 40 weeks on a calendar from a date you know and compare. An ultrasound date from a clinic overrides this estimate.

## Where the Due Date Calculator stops

It is not a medical diagnosis of the due date. A clinic may date the pregnancy from a scan.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
