[Color Blindness Simulator](/color-tools/color-blindness-simulator) is the tool for one job: preview colors as seen with color vision deficiencies. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Color Blindness Simulator

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Preview colors as seen with color vision deficiencies.

Color Blindness Simulator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Color Blindness Simulator

The simulation shows how a given deficiency shifts the colours. It does not diagnose vision. Compare the original and the simulation on the pair you care about, such as red versus green status dots.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the Color Blindness Simulator stops

Color Blindness Simulator does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Color Blindness Simulator do?

It is there to preview colors as seen with color vision deficiencies. The form on the [Color Blindness Simulator](/color-tools/color-blindness-simulator) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Color Blindness Simulator result is usable?

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.
