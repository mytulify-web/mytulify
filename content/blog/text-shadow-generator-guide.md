[Text Shadow Generator](/color-tools/text-shadow-generator) is the tool for one job: create CSS text-shadow effects with live preview. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Text Shadow Generator

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Create CSS text-shadow effects with live preview.

Text Shadow Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Text Shadow Generator

Text shadow sits behind the glyphs. A tight offset and a small blur reads as depth. A huge blur washes the text out.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the Text Shadow Generator stops

Text Shadow Generator does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
