[NDA Generator](/freelancer-tools/nda-generator) is the tool for one job: generate a one-way or mutual non-disclosure agreement and download it as a PDF. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the NDA Generator

Use the figures you and the client already discussed. A blank you leave in will be blank on the PDF.

NDA Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the NDA Generator

A one-way NDA binds one side. A mutual NDA binds both. The names and the definition of confidential information have to match the real project.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Read the export from top to bottom, including the total and the dates. Then send it.

## Where the NDA Generator stops

NDA Generator drafts the document or the number. It is not your lawyer, your accountant, or the client's acceptance.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
