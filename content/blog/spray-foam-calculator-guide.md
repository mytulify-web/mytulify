[Spray Foam Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/spray-foam-calculator) is the tool for one job: convert wall area and foam thickness into board feet, spray foam kits, and material cost. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Spray Foam Calculator

Board feet of foam are square feet of wall times the thickness in inches. Kits are sold by how many board feet they cover.

Spray Foam Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Spray Foam Calculator

A 200 square foot wall at 2 inches is 400 board feet. If a kit covers 600 board feet, one kit is enough for that wall and not enough for a second wall of the same size.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Doubling the thickness should double the board feet and the cost. The wall area should not change.

## Where the Spray Foam Calculator stops

It does not choose open-cell versus closed-cell yield. Use the yield printed on the kit you will buy.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
