## A worked pass with the Client Onboarding Form Generator

Generate a client intake questionnaire as a printable PDF or downloadable HTML form. Put in the numbers and the names from the real job, not a sample, before you export.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.
