[Color Picker](/color-tools/color-picker) is the tool for one job: pick any color and get its HEX, RGB, and HSL values. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Color Picker

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Pick any color and get its HEX, RGB, and HSL values.

Color Picker runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Color Picker

The HEX you copy is the colour on the picker, six digits for opaque RGB. #FFFFFF is white and #000000 is black.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the Color Picker stops

Color Picker does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

It is there to pick any color and get its HEX, RGB, and HSL values. The form on the [Color Picker](/color-tools/color-picker) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Color Picker result is usable?
