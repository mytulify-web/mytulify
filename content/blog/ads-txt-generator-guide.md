[ads.txt Generator](/seo-web-tools/ads-txt-generator) is the tool for one job: create a valid ads.txt file for Google AdSense and ad networks. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the ads.txt Generator

Bring the URL, the HTML, or the keyword list the form asks for.

ads.txt Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the ads.txt Generator

An ads.txt line names the ad system, your publisher id, DIRECT or RESELLER, and a certification id. For Google the system is google.com. Upload the file at the root of the domain that serves the ads.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the real page source or Search Console and confirm the change landed. A result on this page is not a ranking.

## Where the ads.txt Generator stops

ads.txt Generator does not control a search engine. It prepares or checks the input you gave it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
