[Base64 to Image](/image-tools/base64-to-image) is the tool for one job: decode a Base64 string back into a downloadable image. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Base64 to Image

Start from the original BASE64 file, not a screenshot of it.

Base64 to Image runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Base64 to Image

Paste the data URI or the bare Base64. A missing comma after the media type, or a wrapped line, is the usual reason the preview is blank.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download at the size you will use. Check transparency, text, and the file size against the original.

## Where the Base64 to Image stops

Base64 to Image converts the file. It does not recover detail the source never had.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

It is there to decode a Base64 string back into a downloadable image. The form on the [Base64 to Image](/image-tools/base64-to-image) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Base64 to Image result is usable?
