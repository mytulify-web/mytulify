[Business Valuation Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/business-valuation-calculator) is the tool for one job: estimate a small-business value from seller earnings and revenue using multiples you choose. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Business Valuation Calculator

A small-business estimate here is a multiple times seller's discretionary earnings, or a multiple times revenue. You choose the multiples.

Business Valuation Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Business Valuation Calculator

Earnings of 100,000 at a 2.5 multiple is 250,000. Revenue of 500,000 at a 0.6 multiple is 300,000. Those are two opinions, not one fact. The business is not worth both.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Doubling the multiple should double that estimate. If you are comparing earnings and revenue methods, label which multiple belongs to which base.

## Where the Business Valuation Calculator stops

A buyer prices risk, customers, and the owner's role. A multiple from a different industry will mislead you.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
