[Binary to Decimal Converter](/unit-converters/binary-to-decimal-converter) is the tool for one job: convert numbers between binary, decimal, hex, and octal. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Binary to Decimal Converter

Pick the two units before you type the quantity. Convert numbers between binary, decimal, hex, and octal.

Binary to Decimal Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Binary to Decimal Converter

Binary 1010 is decimal 10, and hex A. 255 decimal is FF in hex and 11111111 in binary. A leading zero does not change the value.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Convert the result back the other way. You should land close to the number you started with. A large gap means the units are not the pair you think.

## Where the Binary to Decimal Converter stops

Binary to Decimal Converter converts units. It does not know which unit your drawing, recipe, or ticket required.
