[Angle Converter](/unit-converters/angle-converter) is the tool for one job: convert degrees, radians, gradians, and arcminutes. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Angle Converter

Pick the two units before you type the quantity. Convert degrees, radians, gradians, and arcminutes.

Angle Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Angle Converter

Convert degrees, radians, gradians, and arcminutes. Convert a value you already know in both units, such as a round 1 or 10, and see that the other side matches the definition you trust. Then convert the measurement you actually have.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Convert the result back the other way. You should land close to the number you started with. A large gap means the units are not the pair you think.

## Where the Angle Converter stops

Angle Converter converts units. It does not know which unit your drawing, recipe, or ticket required.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
