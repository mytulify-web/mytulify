[Analogous Color Generator](/color-tools/analogous-color-generator) is the tool for one job: generate analogous color schemes from a base color. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Analogous Color Generator

Have the colour as a HEX, an RGB, or the two colours the form names. Generate analogous color schemes from a base color.

Analogous Color Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Analogous Color Generator

Analogous hues sit beside the base hue, often 30 degrees away. They look related. If one swatch is the opposite hue, the rule is wrong.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Paste the result back into the reverse converter when one exists. You should land near the colour you started with.

## Where the Analogous Color Generator stops

Analogous Color Generator does the colour math on the values you type. It does not see the rest of your page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

It is there to generate analogous color schemes from a base color. The form on the [Analogous Color Generator](/color-tools/analogous-color-generator) page is the place you do that.
