[Related Keywords Generator](/seo-web-tools/related-keywords-generator) is the tool for one job: expand seed keywords into questions, commercial modifiers, long-tail phrases, and combinations for research. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Related Keywords Generator

Bring the URL, the HTML, or the keyword list the form asks for.

Related Keywords Generator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Related Keywords Generator

Expand seed keywords into questions, commercial modifiers, long-tail phrases, and combinations for research. Use a real URL or a real draft. A made-up example only describes the example.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the real page source or Search Console and confirm the change landed. A result on this page is not a ranking.

## Where the Related Keywords Generator stops

Related Keywords Generator does not control a search engine. It prepares or checks the input you gave it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
