[Excel to PDF](/pdf-tools/excel-to-pdf) is the tool for one job: convert CSV exports from Excel into PDF files. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Excel to PDF

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Convert CSV exports from Excel into PDF files.

Excel to PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Excel to PDF

This path expects a CSV export, not a live .xlsx workbook with several sheets. Export the sheet you mean, then convert that file.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Excel to PDF stops

Excel to PDF writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
