[Timestamp Converter](/developer-tools/timestamp-converter) is the tool for one job: convert Unix epoch timestamps to and from human-readable dates. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Timestamp Converter

Paste a real snippet, not a description of the snippet, unless the tool asks you to describe one.

Timestamp Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Timestamp Converter

Unix time is seconds since 1970-01-01 UTC, unless the field says milliseconds. A 13-digit number is milliseconds. A 10-digit number is seconds. The human date should be in the time zone the page names.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Diff the output against the input for a formatter. For a generator, run the result in the real tool that will consume it.

## Where the Timestamp Converter stops

Timestamp Converter does not see your repository, your server, or the version of the library on your machine.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Timestamp Converter do?

It is there to convert Unix epoch timestamps to and from human-readable dates. The form on the [Timestamp Converter](/developer-tools/timestamp-converter) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Timestamp Converter result is usable?

Diff the output against the input for a formatter. For a generator, run the result in the real tool that will consume it.
