[Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator](/health-tools/waist-to-hip-ratio-calculator) is the tool for one job: calculate your waist-to-hip ratio and see your health-risk category by sex. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator

The ratio is waist circumference divided by hip circumference. Measure the waist at the narrowest point or at the navel, the same way each time, and the hips at the widest point.

Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator

A waist of 80 cm and hips of 100 cm is 0.80. Risk categories differ for men and women. A ratio that is higher means more of the circumference is at the waist.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

If you swap waist and hip, the ratio jumps above 1 for most people. That is a clue the fields are reversed.

## Where the Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator stops

The category is a screening chart, not a diagnosis.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
