[Split PDF in Half](/pdf-tools/split-pdf-in-half) is the tool for one job: cut each PDF page vertically or horizontally into two. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Split PDF in Half

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Cut each PDF page vertically or horizontally into two.

Split PDF in Half runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Split PDF in Half

Each page is cut on a vertical or horizontal line into two pages. A two-up scan of a book becomes one page per side. The cut is in the middle unless you move it.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Split PDF in Half stops

Split PDF in Half writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
