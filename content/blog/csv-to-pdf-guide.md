[CSV to PDF](/pdf-tools/csv-to-pdf) is the tool for one job: turn CSV spreadsheet data into a readable PDF. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the CSV to PDF

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Turn CSV spreadsheet data into a readable PDF.

CSV to PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the CSV to PDF

Rows and columns become a table on the page. A wide sheet will wrap or shrink. Check a row in the middle, not only the header.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the CSV to PDF stops

CSV to PDF writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
