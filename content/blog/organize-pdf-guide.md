[Organize PDF](/pdf-tools/organize-pdf) is the tool for one job: reorder, rearrange, and sort PDF pages by drag and drop. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Organize PDF

Use the PDF you mean to change, and read the page numbers the viewer shows. Reorder, rearrange, and sort PDF pages by drag and drop.

Organize PDF runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Organize PDF

Drag order is the new page order. A thumbnail that looks similar is still a different page. Check the first and last page after you save.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

## Where the Organize PDF stops

Organize PDF writes a new file. The original stays where it was until you delete it. A scan remains a picture unless this page says it reads text.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the Organize PDF do?

It is there to reorder, rearrange, and sort PDF pages by drag and drop. The form on the [Organize PDF](/pdf-tools/organize-pdf) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Organize PDF result is usable?

Open the download and check page count, order, and one page you care about. If text used to be selectable, try to select it again.

### What should I not trust the Organize PDF to decide?
