## A worked pass with the Inches to CM Converter

One inch is exactly 2.54 centimetres. Twelve inches is 30.48 cm. Type 1 and confirm 2.54 before you convert a measurement from a drawing.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.
