[Cooking Measurement Converter](/unit-converters/cooking-measurement-converter) is the tool for one job: convert cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, grams, and ounces for recipes. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Cooking Measurement Converter

Pick the two units before you type the quantity. Convert cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, grams, and ounces for recipes.

Cooking Measurement Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Cooking Measurement Converter

A US cup is about 237 ml. A tablespoon is 3 teaspoons, about 15 ml. Grams depend on the ingredient. Flour and water do not weigh the same per cup. Convert volume to volume unless the form names the ingredient.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Convert the result back the other way. You should land close to the number you started with. A large gap means the units are not the pair you think.

## Where the Cooking Measurement Converter stops

Cooking Measurement Converter converts units. It does not know which unit your drawing, recipe, or ticket required.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
