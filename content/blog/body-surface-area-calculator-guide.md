[Body Surface Area Calculator](/health-tools/body-surface-area-calculator) is the tool for one job: compute body surface area with the Mosteller and Du Bois formulas side by side. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Body Surface Area Calculator

Mosteller's formula is the square root of (height in cm times weight in kg, divided by 3600). Du Bois is an older formula with a different curve. They should be close, not identical.

Body Surface Area Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Body Surface Area Calculator

For 170 cm and 70 kg, Mosteller's area is the square root of (170 × 70 / 3600), which is the square root of about 3.31, about 1.82 square metres.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Both formulas should rise if weight rises. If one of them does not, that formula's inputs were left in the wrong unit.

## Where the Body Surface Area Calculator stops

Drug dosing from body surface area is a clinician's decision. Do not dose from this page.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
