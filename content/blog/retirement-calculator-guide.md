[Retirement Calculator](/home-trade-calculators/retirement-calculator) is the tool for one job: project retirement savings from a current balance, monthly contribution, return, and years. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Retirement Calculator

The projection compounds the current balance and adds each monthly contribution at the annual return you type, for the years you type.

Retirement Calculator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Retirement Calculator

Contributions matter more when the balance is small. The return matters more when the balance is large. Run it once with contributions and once without to see which one is doing the work.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

At a 0% return, the ending balance should equal the start plus every contribution, with no growth.

## Where the Retirement Calculator stops

It does not model a crash, a pension, Social Security, or the tax on withdrawals.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
