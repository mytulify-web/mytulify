[Density Converter](/unit-converters/density-converter) is the tool for one job: convert kg/m3, g/cm3, lb/ft3, and other density units. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Density Converter

Pick the two units before you type the quantity. Convert kg/m3, g/cm3, lb/ft3, and other density units.

Density Converter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Density Converter

Convert kg/m3, g/cm3, lb/ft3, and other density units. Convert a value you already know in both units, such as a round 1 or 10, and see that the other side matches the definition you trust. Then convert the measurement you actually have.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Convert the result back the other way. You should land close to the number you started with. A large gap means the units are not the pair you think.

## Where the Density Converter stops

Density Converter converts units. It does not know which unit your drawing, recipe, or ticket required.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.
