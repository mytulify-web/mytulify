## What to bring to the LinkedIn Text Formatter

Paste the caption, the size, or the mockup text you actually plan to publish.

LinkedIn Text Formatter runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the LinkedIn Text Formatter

Add bold/italic Unicode formatting to LinkedIn posts. The preview matches the text you typed. Read it once as a stranger would.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Check the character count and the line breaks in the preview. Then paste into the network and look again. The network can still wrap differently.

## Where the LinkedIn Text Formatter stops

LinkedIn Text Formatter does not post for you. A mockup is a picture, not a live post.
