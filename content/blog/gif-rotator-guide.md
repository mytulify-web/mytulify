[GIF Rotator](/image-tools/gif-rotator) is the tool for one job: rotate or flip animated GIFs on every frame. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the GIF Rotator

Use the original image. Rotate or flip animated GIFs on every frame.

GIF Rotator runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the GIF Rotator

Rotation applies to every frame, so the motion still lines up. A 90 degree turn swaps the width and height of the animation.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

## Where the GIF Rotator stops

GIF Rotator changes the copy you download. It does not edit the file still sitting in your folder until you replace it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

### What does the GIF Rotator do?

It is there to rotate or flip animated GIFs on every frame. The form on the [GIF Rotator](/image-tools/gif-rotator) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the GIF Rotator result is usable?

Compare the download with the original at 100% zoom on the part you care about: a face, a line of text, or the edge of the crop.

### What should I not trust the GIF Rotator to decide?
