[Search Intent Classifier](/seo-web-tools/search-intent-classifier) is the tool for one job: classify keywords as informational, commercial, transactional, or navigational with page-type suggestions. This guide covers the inputs to gather, one pass you can check, and the point where the result stops being enough.

## What to bring to the Search Intent Classifier

Bring the URL, the HTML, or the keyword list the form asks for.

Search Intent Classifier runs in your browser. The file or the figures stay on your device.

## A worked pass with the Search Intent Classifier

Classify keywords as informational, commercial, transactional, or navigational with page-type suggestions. Use a real URL or a real draft. A made-up example only describes the example.

Do that pass with a case you already understand. Then replace it with the real case. Changing one input at a time is how you see which field actually moved the result.

## How to check the result

Open the real page source or Search Console and confirm the change landed. A result on this page is not a ranking.

## Where the Search Intent Classifier stops

Search Intent Classifier does not control a search engine. It prepares or checks the input you gave it.

If your decision depends on a rule this page does not mention, a lender, a clinic, a teacher, a client, or the vendor of the file, use their rule. Keep this result as the draft you compare against that rule.

## Questions

It is there to classify keywords as informational, commercial, transactional, or navigational with page-type suggestions. The form on the [Search Intent Classifier](/seo-web-tools/search-intent-classifier) page is the place you do that.

### How do I know the Search Intent Classifier result is usable?

Open the real page source or Search Console and confirm the change landed. A result on this page is not a ranking.
